# Hyundai – 2026 Warranty Policy and Procedures Manual §5.1, 5.8, 5.10 (summary)

Source: 2026 Hyundai Warranty Policy and Procedures Manual (HMA, dated January 1, 2026; form SVC-1245), plus the HMA Dealer Portal message "Effective Immediately - Update to Warranty P&P labor rate increase policy, section 5.10.3" (published 9/15/2023, last modified 3/14/2024, no expiration), received Nov 2026. Copyrighted dealer document – summary and short quotes only; originals are not stored in the repo. The 2026 change log lists no revisions to 5.1, 5.8 or 5.10.

## Key points
- 5.10.3 and the portal message: Hyundai's own process caps approval at "a maximum of 5% less than the average RO labor dollars per hour calculated in the 25 RO survey," and may approve less case by case based on local market and the total year-over-year increase. Requests above that cap are "returned with directions to submit under dealer's state law guidelines" (portal message).
- State law is a separate track: 5.10.3 requests "do not constitute labor rate requests under applicable state law procedures" and "are not governed by state law"; "Nothing in this policy prohibits a dealer" from having its rate set under state law, and statutory requests "shall be governed by the applicable state law" (5.10.3, p.119–120).
- 5.8 (p.114): labor = approved warranty labor rate in effect on the repair date x Labor Time Standard (LTS) time. Actual-time (TT) operations need Service Manager sign-off before work starts. No "unless state law" qualifier in 5.8 itself.
- 5.1 (p.86): parts = dealer net price on the RO open date + 40% "parts handling allowance" (2016–2026 MY and over-the-counter claims), "Unless otherwise required by applicable state law." Buy-sell dealers get the lower of the seller's allowance or the state median. Cores: allowance applies to dealer net of the assembly, not the core value (5.1.1).
- No Hyundai procedure exists to raise the parts allowance – it is a flat 40%. A parts markup increase is a state-statute filing only.

## 5.10 procedure (existing dealer)
- Considerations (5.10): current actual retail labor rate, reasonableness vs. local competition/market, and year-over-year increases.
- Frequency (5.10.1): once 12 months have passed since the last approved rate. Only the Dealer Principal or GM may sign the request.
- Packet (5.10.3): Warranty Labor Rate Request/Competitive Labor Rate Evaluation Form (SVC-1371), Warranty Labor Rate Survey (SVC-1370), Warranty Labor Establishment Checklist (SVC-1057), and the 25 most current sequentially numbered qualified Hyundai customer-pay ROs, qualifying lines highlighted (hours and dollars). Forms are on HyundaiDealer.com > Service > Dealer Resources > Documents Library > Warranty. Competitor rates may not come from same-ownership-group dealers.
- Who picks the ROs: dealer – the 25 most current, in sequence, Hyundai vehicles only. RO open date goes in the survey (5.10.5). Sum qualifying lines per RO onto one survey line (5.10.6 example).
- Routing: dealer -> DPSM (validates highlighted hours/dollars against the survey) -> Regional Warranty Manager -> National Warranty Department for final approval; National notifies the region. No stated turnaround or effective-date rule for existing dealers (new dealers: allow 30+ days; effective on activation, 5.10.2).
- Returned if: ROs out of sequence, forms not typed, hours handwritten, non-qualified ROs included, or ROs do not support the requested rate.
- Verification (5.10.5): approved rate may not exceed the posted retail rate; retail rate must be reasonable and consistent with local trade practices; warranty labor must be a reasonable share of CP + warranty labor sales "as determined by HMA." Posting rules: rate displayed at write-up and cashier in 2-inch lettering, wording prescribed for clock-time, flat-rate manual, discounted/senior, skill-level and team systems; rate pre-printed on every CP RO; hours in tenths, typed.
- Chargeback (5.10.5): if the regional review finds discrepancies, the difference between the verified and approved rate is charged back "for all labor hours approved under the unsubstantiated warranty labor rate," and the verified rate becomes the new rate. No look-back limit stated.

## RO exclusions (5.10.6) – highlights
- Excluded: repairs with discounts; all factory maintenance (oil, filters, all belts, spark plugs, tune-ups, fluid flushes); internal, extended warranty/service contract, insurance; anything not fully customer paid; goodwill (including partial dealer absorption); abuse; brake pads (unless part of a larger repair such as rotors); A/C evac and recharge; **wheel balance and alignment**; tire service; reconditioning; wiper blades; non-Hyundai vehicles; state safety/smog inspections; all service specials; menu repairs; fleet variable rates.
- Contradiction to flag: the service-special note says ongoing menu items "are not considered service specials," yet menu repairs are a separate exclusion. List is "not all-inclusive and is subject to change."
- Discount treatment: any discounted repair is excluded outright (no proportional allocation as in Toyota).

## Claims, audits, records
- Claim deadline (6.0, p.130): 30 days from RO close "unless state law provides otherwise"; returned claims 30 days from return. Rentals/SRC submitted with the repair claim, 30-day rule, "Unless otherwise required by applicable state law" (5.0.8).
- Audits (Sales and Service Agreement 14.D; 6.8–6.9): HMA may audit all records at reasonable times; chargebacks for improper or unsubstantiated claims. No look-back window stated; records kept 5 years from claim payment (6.2.1, 14.C).
- HMA "reserves the right to furnish the final decisions in all warranty matters" (Section 4, Judgement of Warranty Matters). Agreement 20.D waives jury trial "to the extent permitted by law."

## Portal / 2FA claim (vendor)
- Not supported by these sources. The 2026 manual describes a paper/PDF packet (typed forms plus RO copies) delivered to the DPSM and region; it does not mention a portal upload, store credentials or two-factor authentication for labor rate requests. The portal page is only a policy message, not a submission tool. Confirm with the DPSM/Regional Warranty Manager before relying on either version; a newer process may exist outside the manual.
