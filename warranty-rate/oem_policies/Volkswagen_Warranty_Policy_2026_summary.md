# Volkswagen – 2026 Warranty Policies and Procedures Manual §2.2.1, 2.4, 2.6 (summary)

Source: Volkswagen of America Warranty Policies and Procedures Manual, 2026 edition (page footers dated 02/2026; © 2026), reviewed Nov 2026. Part of the VW Dealer Agreement Standard Provisions (Foreword, p. 2). Copyrighted dealer document – summary and short quotes only; originals are not stored in the repo. The rate-increase forms themselves (Repair Order Analysis, Dealer Request for Warranty Labor Rate Increase) are on WISE (Resource Center > Forms > Labor Rate) and are not in the manual.

## Key points
- Foreword (p. 2): all rights and obligations "are subject to any applicable state or federal law or regulation." Ch. 2 intro (p. 34): the manual applies "Unless specifically contradicted by relevant law."
- 2.4.2.2 (p. 48): two labor rate increase options "(unless otherwise addressed by applicable law and regulations)": Option 1 CPI, Option 2 effective retail rate. California dealers are excluded from VW's process entirely and file "in accordance with the prevailing state statutes."
- 2.4.2 (p. 45): labor = repair time x the dealer's approved warranty labor rate in effect on the repair date. Time = Elsa flat-rate SRTs; actual clocked time (A-time) only for exceptional conditions and diagnosis (01320000). Time recorded in hundredths of an hour ("time units"). No "straight time" language.
- 2.4.1.1 (p. 43): parts for MY2003+ reimbursed at **MSRP**; MY2002-and-older and over-the-counter stock parts at Dealer Cost + 40%, capped at MSRP. Shop supplies and handling are not separately reimbursed ("included in the parts reimbursement").
- **No parts markup change procedure.** The only reference is the contact line in 2.4.2.2 (p. 49): send "documentation and inquiries regarding Dealer reimbursement for labor rate & parts mark-up" to the Warranty Labor Rate Specialist (warrantylaborrate@vw.com, fax 248-754-4663, or mail to Auburn Hills). A parts markup increase would go there under the state statute.
- 2.4.3 (p. 52): sublet at dealer cost, no markup; sublet labor capped at Elsa SRT x warranty labor rate.

## Option 1 – CPI program (2.4.2.2, pp. 49–50)
- Three-year commitment; automatic increase each June 1 by CPI-U (unadjusted, 12 months ended February, bls.gov).
- Enroll through WISE during open enrollment: starts ~1st working day after the February CPI release (generally Mar 15–31) and runs six weeks. Dealer Principal / authorized rep named in the Dealer Agreement must sign.
- First increase may be prorated by months since the last increase. Annual increase capped at **4%**; no decrease if CPI falls.
- While enrolled, Option 1 is "the only method" for a labor rate increase until the term ends. No automatic re-enrollment. (Check whether any store is enrolled before filing under a statute.)

## Option 2 – effective retail rate (2.4.2.2, pp. 50–52)
- Written request to the Warranty Labor Rate Specialist for re-evaluation based on the current retail rate, "except where otherwise specified by State law."
- Frequency: once per 12 months from the effective date of the last adjustment. Requests accepted June 2–March 31; not processed in April or May. A rejection bars a new request for 90 days.
- Cap: requested rate may not exceed the effective retail rate; the offer is the calculated effective rate "not to exceed the posted labor rate." Dealer must notify VW within 10 days if its customer-pay effective rate is reduced.
- Sample: **30 sequential** non-warranty ROs for warranty-like qualifying repairs, paid by a retail customer, closed by the submission date, and "not more than 12 months before the submission." The manual does not assign the start RO – the dealer picks the window. All ROs in the sequence (qualifying and non-qualifying, coded B, C, F, I, S, O, P, V, W, EM) are listed and submitted as legible PDFs; no handwritten changes.
- Hours: Elsa labor ops or the dealer's own customer labor times, used consistently. Hours = time units billed to the customer.
- Rate = total labor charged / total time units charged. Labor $ is "after any discounts" from the RO (discounts included, not added back).
- Exclusions (2(b)): fleet / government / third-party-payor volume discounts (service contracts, insurance, body shops, sublet); employee-owned vehicles; collision, road hazard, elements, vandalism, theft, **rodent** and other negligence damage; reconditioning; state emission/safety inspections; pre-sale inspections; goodwill/policy; accessories and non-VW parts; maintenance (LOF, packages, plugs, belts, fluid services); preventive maintenance (injector/carbon cleaning, additives); non-warranty fluids, filters, fuses, bulbs, belts; keys/transmitters without defect; **batteries, brakes (pads, rotors, shoes, drums), tires, fuses, bulbs, wheel alignment.**
- Required: Dealer Request for Warranty Labor Rate Increase form signed by the Dealer Principal / authorized rep (e.g., GM named in the Dealer Agreement) with email; Repair Order Analysis; photo of the posted labor rate sign; copy of any retail rate disclosure made to a government agency (e.g., state DMV).
- Posted rate sign (both options): at least 20"x20", 1¼" letters, stating the customer hourly rate and the labor time guide used; image must be submitted with the forms. Manual says to check state/local rules.
- "Request for Reimbursement of Warranty Labor at Retail Rate" page: "Applicable only in States where law requires reimbursement of warranty labor at retail rate." Lists effective retail, posted, current warranty and requested rates.
- No competitive/market survey requirement and no stated reasonableness test (unlike Honda/Toyota). VW "may or may not grant" the request and may propose a different rate if documentation doesn't support the request.
- Effective date: communicated after the Dealer Principal agrees to the new rate and after system updates. No fixed number of days. Applies to repairs on or after that date; no retroactive pay for earlier repairs.
- Verification: VW may request more support at any time; if non-compliant, the former rate may be reinstated and the dealer "debited retroactively for any warranty over-payment." Appeal in writing to the Labor Rate Specialist with the decision letter.
- New dealership: rate set by the Specialist (factors include existing VW dealers' rates). Buy-sell: buyer assumes the outgoing dealer's rate until it qualifies for an increase.
- Redact sensitive PII from all submitted ROs.

## Claims, audits, chargebacks
- 2.2.1 (p. 38): submission deadline runs from RO close date; late claims are blocked in SAGA. **AZ, ID, NV, UT: 30 days. WA: 90 days.** "Time limitations may be subject to change."
- 2.3.5 (p. 41): keep warranty and non-warranty documentation at least 2 years after payment, or longer if law requires.
- 2.6 (p. 58): VW may audit all records; unsupported claims may be denied or charged back under the manual edition in effect at the time of repair. Written notice before audits. The manual states no look-back limit – state statutes govern.
- General: VW "reserves the right to charge back or deny claims" not in compliance with the manual and other warranty publications (p. 33).
