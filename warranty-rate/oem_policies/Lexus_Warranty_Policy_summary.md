# Lexus – Warranty Policy and Procedures Manual §7.1–7.9 (summary)

Source: Lexus Warranty Policy and Procedures Manual (TMS, 296-page PDF, received Nov 2026). The manual has no single edition date; each policy carries its own revision date. Most recent pages are 7/25 (8.4); PDF last modified 7/31/2025. Revision dates for the policies used here: 7.1, 7.2, 7.3 (7/24); 7.4 (10/24); 7.6 (3/22); 7.9 (3/22); 5.2 (11/21); 8.1 (9/20). Page numbers below are PDF pages. Copyrighted dealer document – summary and short quotes only; originals are not stored in the repo. Relevant store: Findlay Lexus Spokane (WA, RCW 46.96.105).

## Key points
- 7.1 (p.186): labor = Lexus flat rate time (straight time on "Others 99" op codes) x the dealer's "current authorized warranty labor rate," which "must be approved in advance." UFRM used "except as required by State Law."
- 7.6 (p.201): parts = **MSRP on the repair date** (over-the-counter parts: dealer cost + 30%). Reman parts at exchange price (MSRP minus core); core not reimbursed by warranty. Disposal fees not reimbursed. Goodwill claims (5.14, p.156) are paid on the same basis, and "Additional labor, parts, and/or sublet mark-up, are not allowed."
- No Lexus procedure exists to change parts reimbursement – it is MSRP by policy. A parts markup increase is a state-statute filing only. (Repo history: Lexus Spokane already has a statutory parts markup, approved 3/24/2026, per `config/history_cycles.json`.)
- Sublet (7.7): 100% of dealer cost, no markup.
- Policy 7.5 (Formula Rate) was eliminated 9/97.

## Options (7.3 / 7.4) – same structure as Toyota
- **Option A – CPI program:** automatic adjustment on the dealer's anniversary date by calendar-year CPI-U (all items, not seasonally adjusted), 2% minimum when CPI-U is 0–2%, initial 3-year term. Enroll with the CPI Labor Rate Dealer Participation Agreement (Claims Processing & Resource Center > Forms); Area Office reviews, TMS approves. Adjustments between CPI-U announcement dates (around Jan 12) use the prior year's CPI-U; requests received 12/15–1/15 take effect 1/20 (7.4, p.191–192). Renewal/opt-out terms are in the agreement, not the manual. A buy/sell cancels the seller's enrollment (7.2).
- **Option B – Effective labor rate:** Lexus Electronic Labor Rate Request Worksheet (Excel, all tabs, original source file) + Labor Rate Request Checklist + competitive market survey, submitted electronically.

## Option B mechanics (7.3 / 7.4, p.189–199)
- Start: dealer sends the "Start Request" tab to the Area Office; the Area Office gives a written start date (an RO open date) "prior to the notification date." Same as Toyota.
- Sample: from the start date, record each successive qualified repair until **30 qualified Lexus customer-pay ROs**; the dealer "shall not bypass or omit any qualified RO." Submit every numerically consecutive RO in the range, any make or pay type (customer pay, warranty, internal). Same as Toyota.
- Window: customer-pay ROs within the most recent **120 days**; the timeline graphic (7.3, p.190) reads "within 120 days of the notification date," and the completed request must reach TMS **within 60 days of the notification date**. The 60-day submission limit is not in our Toyota summary.
- RO copies: all pages of the accounting copy of each qualified RO, with labor hours, dollars and description highlighted. Hours = hours billed to the customer, not flag time; a DMS report of hours by RO and line may substitute; time punches are not accepted. Dealer principal signs the worksheet.
- ELR excludes warranty, goodwill, internal and non-Lexus vehicles.
- Discounts: allocated proportionally to all customer-paid labor and parts on the RO, then reduce the qualified repair, even if the discount is tied to a specific job. Worksheet does the math. Same as Toyota.
- Competitive survey (differs from Toyota's 2 import / 2 domestic): nearest **4 competitive dealers, minimum 1 domestic** (Cadillac, Lincoln; imports Acura, Audi, BMW, Infiniti, Land Rover, Mercedes-Benz, Volvo). No dealers the Dealer Principal has an interest in; include a major independent shop "if applicable." The survey is described as a reasonableness "check," not a cap.
- Routing: Area DSPM (must review excluded ROs so no qualified repairs were missed) -> TMS for analysis and approval. TMS checks forms/authorizations, 120-day window and highlighting, then whether the worksheet supports the rate. Lexus notifies the Area Office **within 30 days of complete documentation**; the Area Office tells the dealer the rate and effective date.
- Effective date: repairs on or after the effective date; earlier repairs paid at the prior rate regardless of claim date. No rule says how the effective date is set.
- Cap: no increase above the "Current Retail Labor Rate ($)" on the request form (7.3).
- Frequency: once per rolling 12 months from the effective date of the last increase. Getting a start date and not finishing, or declining the offered rate, can block a new start-date request for 12 months from the prior request (7.3). Same as Toyota.
- Missed years: if a dealer does not apply for 3+ years, Lexus may limit the increase to the sum of the missed years' CPI-U changes (7.3, p.190).
- Labor time standards: Lexus does not require any particular labor guide or method for retail hours (7.4, p.198).
- Limitations (apply to Options A and B), each "unless prohibited by state law": a warranty audit within 6 months can block an increase; dealers on Level 2/3 of the Warranty Expense Control Program / WPI are ineligible until 2 months off the program (reconsideration possible); minimum technical training/certification required (7.4, p.198–199).
- Chargeback: if an inspection finds the warranty rate above the current effective retail labor rate, Lexus may charge back the difference and reset the rate, "unless prohibited by state law" (7.2, 7.4). No look-back period stated.

## Non-qualified repairs (7.4 table, p.195–198) – highlights and Toyota differences
- Excluded: scheduled maintenance per the Lexus Scheduled Maintenance Guide (interval services, air/cabin filters, transmission/brake/coolant services, spark plugs, caliper cleaning), **alignments (2- and 4-wheel)** and **timing/alternator/serpentine belt replacements**; DIO accessories and navigation map updates; battery service without replacement; internal, rental, employee/family and other-auto-business customers; cosmetic and headlight restoration; dealer glass repair and sublet body/glass; undocumented diagnosis; whole RO if any part was extended warranty, insurance or third-party paid; repairs covered by pre-paid maintenance; goodwill (full or partial); hybrid battery cooling/fan service; inspections (appraisals, health checks, pre-purchase, state safety/smog); outside-influence cleaning, flood, rodent nest removal; non-genuine, mixed or used parts; service specials (A/C refresh, brake fluid, differential, decarb, PCV, power steering, MAF, radiator, throttle body/induction/injector); tire work (balancing, lug nuts, nitrogen, rotations, replacement/repair); non-Lexus vehicles; personalization; bulbs, fuses, other belts, wipers.
- Included exceptions: brake pads and rotor turning; battery replacement and cable/clamp; radio/nav data patches; keys/fobs with Lexus part number; dealer glass replacement; mechanical repairs from outside influence; blind-spot/mirror, parking-sensor and rain-sensor calibrations; documented diagnosis (MIL with scan tool, squeak/rattle/leak); LexusCare ROs that include qualified repairs; rodent-damage repairs; Lexus-certified reman parts, customer-supplied Lexus parts, special-order parts, TSIB-directed aftermarket parts; TPMS (unless tied to recent tire work) and wheel studs; clutch. "All other services are to be included."
- Differences from Toyota 7.4: Toyota includes alignments and timing/alternator/serpentine belts as exceptions; **Lexus excludes both** (listed under scheduled maintenance). Lexus adds battery replacement, the service-specials category, pre-paid maintenance and the LexusCare inclusion.

## State-law language
- "Unless prohibited by state law" qualifies the audit, WPI and training limits and the rate chargeback (7.4). UFRM use is "except as required by State Law" (7.1). Chargebacks for unsupported time punches are "(Subject to applicable state law)" (8.14); transmission-return debits "where consistent with state law" (5.17).
- 7.3 adds that the warranty rate "does not relieve the dealer of the obligation to comply with applicable federal, state, or local laws."
- Unlike Hyundai and Honda, the manual has **no statement that a dealer may file under state statute instead** and no statute-only request form. The claim deadline, parts basis and audit rights carry no state-law qualifier.

## Claims, audits, records
- Claim deadline (5.2, p.128): claims must be "received by Lexus within 30 days of the repair date"; after 30 days DSPM authorization is needed (5.3); no claims for repairs over 6 months old, even with authorization. Claims left in New Interface/Ready/Not Approved/Error status 90+ days are auto-deleted. No state-law qualifier (WA allows 90 days, RCW 46.96.105(2)).
- Audits (8.3, p.214; Lexus Dealer Agreement): Lexus may examine, audit and reproduce all service records "at all reasonable times"; unsubstantiated or non-compliant claims may be charged back. 7.9 (p.210): paid claims with inaccurate or misleading information can be charged back (no-defect parts, over-repair, non-genuine parts, documentation failures). **No look-back limit stated** (WA: 9 months after payment, longer for fraud).
- Records (8.1, p.211): keep warranty documentation at least 3 years from the repair date, retrievable by VIN and RO; produce 40 paper ROs per hour; illegible or missing records are charged back.

## Open items for a WA statutory filing
- Confirm whether Lexus Spokane is enrolled in Option A (CPI) and its term and exit terms before filing under RCW 46.96.105.
- The WA labor standard (retail rate, refusable only if it "unreasonably exceeds" all market dealers) differs from Lexus' 30-RO ELR method, the 4-dealer competitive survey, the alignment/belt exclusions and the audit/WPI/training blocks. Those blocks are qualified "unless prohibited by state law"; the manual does not say how Lexus handles a statutory filing.
- Parts: Lexus pays MSRP by policy; WA allows cost + declared average markup (100 sequential CP ROs or 90 days, whichever is less, within 180 days), and a manufacturer "may not require" another methodology.
