# Acura – 2026 Dealer Operations Manual §5, §6.1, 6.2, 6.15 (summary)

Source: 2026 Acura Dealer Operations Manual (DOM), © American Honda Motor Co., published January 2026, revised Feb/May/Jun/Sep 2026 (cover "Rev 09/2026"), and the Acura Warranty Labor Rate Change forms BWL 9361-26806, MWL 4990 8209, AWL 3069 4735 (all rev 10/16/2025), received Nov 2026. Copyrighted dealer document – summary and short quotes only; originals are not stored in the repo. Page numbers are DOM page numbers.

## Key points
- 6.15 (p. 96) opens with the same language as Honda: "Your state likely has a statute that provides a procedure for establishing your warranty labor reimbursement rate. However, as an alternative to the procedure outlined in the applicable state statute, you may submit a request ... pursuant to this Section." Acura's method is an alternative, not a replacement.
- American Honda reimburses Acura warranty labor at the dealer's "effective actual retail labor rate," which must be reasonable, consistent with local trade practices, and is verified by American Honda.
- 6.1 (p. 91): labor = Acura flat-rate time x authorized warranty labor rate, "unless otherwise required by state law." Straight time only where no flat-rate op exists; Service Manager approves all straight time, DPSM approves over 1.0 hr.
- 6.2 (p. 91): parts = dealer net + 40% handling (30% for over-the-counter parts claims). Same as Honda. 6.4 materials (bulletin-directed only) also net + 40%. There is **no OEM procedure for a parts markup change** – a higher parts rate would have to come through the state statute.
- The authorized warranty labor rate also drives Acura Care VSC claims (2.23.6, p. 46), AHM-owned vehicle service (14.3.6–14.3.7: warranty labor rate; parts net + 40%; sublet cost + 10%) and goodwill, which is paid "at warranty cost ... Unless otherwise required by state law" (3.3.1, p. 55).

## 6.15 eligibility and procedure (pp. 96–98)
- Eligible only if iExam 12-month warranty mechanical labor GP% is 80% or lower and American Honda hasn't adjusted the rate under 6.15 in the prior 12 months. Same test as Honda. Covers the "Warranty Service Shop and/or Body Shop Labor Rate."
- Forms (on Acura iN: Service > Warranty > Labor Rate Change Request): Dealer Request for Change or Establishment of a Service Shop Warranty Labor Rate (**BWL 9361-26806** – Acura's own number; Honda uses AWL 3068-26804), Labor Rate Survey (MWL 4990 8209) and Repair Order Evaluation (AWL 3069 4735) – the last two carry the same numbers as Honda's.
- Request form fields: current warranty rate, current effective customer labor rate, requested rate "(Not to exceed current customer labor rate)." Dealer Principal certifies and acknowledges that discrepancies between the established rate and the verified retail rate "will result in chargebacks." Zone Service Manager recommends; National Warranty Manager approves and sets the effective date; "Rate not to be established until all signatures have been obtained."
- Sample: 20 consecutive qualifying client-pay ROs chosen from a group of at least 50 sequentially numbered ROs. More than one repair per RO may be used. The manual doesn't say who picks the range (reads as the dealer). Accounting copies of every RO in the range, including excluded ones, are kept for DPSM review and sent to Zone as a PDF on a memory stick.
- Routing: notify DPSM in writing; DPSM checks forms, the 20 consecutive ROs, the survey and that the rate is "fair, reasonable, and consistent with local trade practices." Don't send to Zone/National before DPSM review. Zone reviews, National gives final approval; an "unreasonable" request is "adjusted and implemented." Dealer notified in writing.
- Effective date: claims with an RO date on or after the approved effective date pay at the new rate; earlier RO dates pay at the old rate. No retroactive pay-back to the request date.
- Rate = total labor charges on the 20 ROs / hours listed on the ROs (or in a dealer report), regardless of pricing strategy (single rate, menu, variable). DPSM may substitute reasonable/customary flat-rate time where none is listed or it's wrong.
- Requirements (6.15.4): requested rate may not exceed the calculated effective retail rate; hours in tenths listed per repair on the accounting copy; retail charges must use a consistently used time source (AHM verifies flat-rate times); warranty labor share of total labor must be reasonable vs. the Zone average.
- Exclusions (6.15.2): advertised service specials (definite start/end dates, usually ~90 days, documented by a flier; ongoing discounted menu pricing is NOT excluded and stays in), non-Acura parts installation, fluid flushes, fleet discounts, labor lines with no parts, diagnostics only (added in 2026), tire replacement, insurance-paid repairs, Owner's Manual / Maintenance Minder / dealer-recommended maintenance, extended service contract repairs. Same list as Honda.
- Survey (6.15.3): competitive brands near the dealership – the manual lists **Lexus, Audi, Mercedes-Benz, Infiniti, BMW and Cadillac** (luxury peers, vs. Honda's mainstream list). Excluded: any dealership with the same or affiliated ownership. The survey form has six "nearest dealer" slots with retail labor rate and phone.

## Verification, chargebacks and claim rules
- 6.15.5–6.15.6: Zone may re-verify after the rate is set, using the same 20-of-50 RO test (verification list also excludes aftermarket-part lines).
- 6.15.7: if the calculated rate is below the requested rate, the rate is set at the calculated rate (if reasonable).
- 6.15.8: discrepancies found on verification can be charged back – the difference between the established and supported rate "for all labor hours" paid during the period of the increased rate, with a letter explaining why.
- 5.2.7 (p. 84): claims submitted daily; late claims need DPSM authorization; returned claims resubmitted within 10 days; AHM "will not honor claims later than 45 days from the last submittal date unless otherwise required by state law."
- 5.3 / 5.5 (pp. 85–87): keep all warranty claim evidence at least 3 years from payment; AHM may audit claims paid in that 3-year window at any reasonable business hour, interview staff, and debit unsubstantiated claims (claim debit, parts account debit or check). Missing or illegible records "may result in a full chargeback." No separate look-back limit for the labor-rate chargeback in 6.15.8 beyond "the period of the increased ... rate."
- 1.2: AHM may change policies on issuance; the online manual controls. 1.4: the DOM is part of Article 12.9 of the current Dealer Agreement (3.9 of the former).

## Differences from Honda SOM 6.15
- Survey peers: Acura lists six luxury brands; Honda lists mainstream brands (Toyota, Nissan, Kia, Hyundai, Chevrolet, Ford, Mazda, Subaru, Mitsubishi).
- Request form number BWL 9361-26806 (Acura) vs. AWL 3068-26804 (Honda); survey and RO evaluation forms are shared.
- Everything else – state-law alternative language, iExam ≤80% test, once per 12 months, 20-of-50 RO sample, exclusions, DPSM → Zone → National routing, cap at effective rate, chargeback rule, parts at net + 40% / 30% OTC – matches Honda.

## Ambiguities
- The 2026 Summary of Changes says 6.15.3 "Removed 'Luxury Automotive brands'," yet the section still names six luxury brands. Confirm with the DPSM which peers are acceptable before building the survey.
- The request form says it uses "the formula checked above," but has no formula checkboxes (leftover from an older two-option form; 6.15.7's "Option A / Option B" text was removed in 2026).
- 6.15.1 mentions a Body Shop labor rate, but 6.7 says warranty paint repairs are paid only as sublet at the body shop's posted door rate and "Service department warranty labor rates are no longer valid" for sublet paint.
- 5.2.7's "45 days from the last submittal date" is unclear on whether it runs from first submission or last resubmission.
