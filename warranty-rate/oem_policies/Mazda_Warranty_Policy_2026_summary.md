# Mazda – Warranty Policies & Procedures Manual (2/2026 edition, rev. 10/2026) – summary

Source: Mazda Warranty Policies & Procedures Manual, 248 pp., received Nov 2026 (requested from Mazda Henderson). Dealer document – paraphrased summary only; original not stored.

Findlay store: Mazda Henderson (NV). DealerUplift history: one parts cycle, approved (see `history/`).

## Key points
- **Parts (Policy 4.1):** Mazda parts on warranty paid at dealer net on the repair date **+ 40%**, all model years. Reman engines/transmissions at the dealer's current warranty parts markup.
- **Parts markup increase – yes, by state statute:** a dealer may submit a warranty retail parts markup request **once a year, unless the state statute says otherwise**. Signed by the Dealer Principal, with supporting documents; forms and documentation list come from the **region**. Mazda may re-verify the markup at any time and charge back non-conforming claims. No sample size or method in the manual – the state statute governs.
- **Labor (Policies 4.2, 4.3):** warranty labor rate (WLR) is based on the dealer's **effective retail labor rate** (customer-pay labor dollars / labor hours). The WLR can never exceed the effective retail rate.
- **Labor rate request process (4.3):**
  - Once every 12 months from the last increase; earlier (not under 6 months) for special circumstances such as union contracts or state statute.
  - Forms from the **District Service Manager (DSM)**: (1) Warranty Labor Rate Request Form, (2) Warranty/Retail Labor Rate Survey.
  - Market survey: **at least six competing makes**, verified by the DSM; substitutions allowed for similar-class makes; at most two franchises from any one dealership; the requesting dealer's own other franchises count once; keep the same surveyed dealers as prior surveys; "selection procedure may be defined by state or local laws."
  - If the request exceeds the survey's average retail rate: **Repair Order Survey** (form from DSM) sent to Mazda Warranty Operations with copies of ROs **in sequential order, including non-qualifying ROs**. Mazda responds with the effective labor rate. Sample size and look-back are not stated.
  - Decision within **30 days** of receiving complete forms; new rate applies to repairs on/after the effective date (no retro).
  - Appeal within 30 days via the DSM; pending appeal the rate moves to the market average or RO-survey rate, capped at the retail rate.
  - Dealer must apply the retail rate consistently with a recognized time guide (Warranty Wizard, Mitchell, Motor, Chilton); advertised service specials should not run over 90 days (or as allowed by law). Dealer must report any decrease in its effective retail rate.
- **State law:** the manual expressly ties the parts markup route to state statute, allows earlier labor requests for statutory reasons, and requires compliance with the dealer's state statute.

## Records and audits
- Replaced warranty parts kept 30 days (some longer until on the scrap report); requested parts must arrive within 14 days or face debit.
- PDI documentation kept 3 years. No general claim look-back period found in the sections searched.

## Fit for Mazda Henderson (NV)
- Nevada has no statutory method (retail floor, NRS 482.36385). For parts, Mazda defers to the statute; for labor, the market survey and the "not above effective retail" cap apply.
- Get from the DSM / region: WLR Request Form, Warranty/Retail Labor Rate Survey, Repair Order Survey Form, and the parts markup request forms.
