# Honda – 2026 Service Operations Manual §6.1, 6.2, 6.15 (summary)

Source: 2026 Honda Service Operations Manual (SOM) and the Warranty Labor Rate Change forms AWL 3068-26804, MWL 4990 8209, AWL 3069 4735 (rev 10/16/2025), received Nov 2026. Copyrighted dealer document – summary and short quotes only; originals are not stored in the repo.

## Key points
- 6.15 opens: "Your state likely has a statute that provides a procedure for establishing your warranty labor reimbursement rate. However, as an alternative to the procedure outlined in the applicable state statute, you may submit a request ... pursuant to this Section." Honda's method is an alternative, not a replacement.
- Honda reimburses warranty labor at the dealer's "effective actual retail labor rate," which must be reasonable and consistent with local trade practices.
- 6.1: labor = Honda flat-rate time x authorized warranty labor rate, "unless otherwise required by state law."
- 6.2: parts = dealer net + 40% handling (30% for over-the-counter parts claims).

## 6.15 eligibility and procedure
- Eligible only if iExam 12-month warranty mechanical labor GP% is 80% or lower and Honda hasn't adjusted the rate under 6.15 in the prior 12 months.
- Forms: Dealer Request (AWL 3068-26804), Labor Rate Survey (MWL 4990 8209: Toyota, Nissan, Kia, Hyundai, Chevrolet, Ford, Mazda, Subaru, Mitsubishi; no luxury brands, no affiliated dealers), Repair Order Evaluation (AWL 3069 4735).
- Sample: 20 consecutive qualifying customer-pay ROs chosen from a group of at least 50 sequentially numbered ROs (dealer chooses). Accounting copies of every RO in the range, including excluded ones. DPSM reviews before Zone, then National approves.
- Rate = total labor charges / hours listed on the ROs (or dealer report), regardless of pricing strategy. Honda may adjust flat-rate hours to reasonable/customary.
- Requested rate may not exceed the calculated effective rate. Warranty labor share must be reasonable vs. Zone average.
- Exclusions: advertised service specials (definite start/end dates; ongoing menu pricing is NOT excluded), non-Honda parts, fluid flushes, fleet discounts, labor lines with no parts, diagnostics only, tire replacement, insurance-paid repairs, Owner's Manual / Maintenance Minder / dealer-recommended maintenance, extended service contract repairs.
- 6.15.8: discrepancies found on later verification can be charged back for all warranty hours paid at the higher rate.
