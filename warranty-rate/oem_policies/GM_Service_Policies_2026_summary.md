# GM – Service Policies & Procedures Manual (Jan 1, 2026), Parts & Accessories P&P Manual (2026), DSSA Standard Provisions (2025)

Sources received Nov 2026 (dealer-only documents – paraphrased summary; originals not stored):
- Service Policies and Procedures Manual, publish/effective Jan 1, 2026 (162 pp.) – Article 5.1 is the rate section (pp. 71–74).
- 2026 GM Dealer Parts and Accessories Policies and Procedures Manual (rev. June 3, 2026, 182 pp.).
- Dealer Sales and Service Agreement – 2025 Standard Provisions and Dispute Resolution (GMMS 1013, 47 pp.).

Findlay GM stores: Chevrolet Las Vegas (NV), Cadillac (NV), Buick GMC Prescott (AZ), Chevy GMC / Findlay Motor Company (AZ).

## Key points
- **GM has a written dealer-filed route for both labor and parts, and it mirrors the state-statute method:** 100 sequential customer-pay job cards, repairs completed no more than 180 days before submission, warranty-like repairs only.
- Two labor rate options (5.1.A):
  - **Option A** – rate from the dealer's customer-pay pricing, compared with same line-make dealers in the area; must pass a reasonableness test. Dealer may request an increase **once per year (unless otherwise permitted by law)**. GM may also run its own review **twice a year or per state statute** and adjust the rate (down as well as up).
  - **Option C** – 3-year contract; rate rises every Jan 1 by the prior year's CPI factor, **minimum 2.5%**. The dealer agrees **not to request a labor rate under state law** and not to seek parts markup above GM standard. Any request outside Option C moves the store to Option A **at the rate it had when it first enrolled – all annual increases removed** – and parts markup to GM standard. One increase request per 3-year term. Enrollment only in November, after 12+ months on Option A.
  - New points/buy-sells take the selling dealer's rate (or area average) and start on Option A.
- **Parts handling allowance (5.1.B):** GM pays a standard markup; on request it may be calculated under the state's law. Same 100 sequential / 180 days / warranty-like rules, plus part numbers, dealer cost and customer charge per line, and discounts/coupons clearly shown.
- **Risk in every request:** when a dealer files for parts or labor, GM may run a labor rate analysis on the same job cards and adjust the labor rate at its discretion. A parts filing can reopen the labor rate.
- Reasonableness test: if the requested labor rate or parts allowance is unreasonable vs. same line-make dealers in the area, GM sets it to the **average market rate** (Option C: stays at the prior Option C rate).

## Warranty-like definition (same list for Option A, Option C and parts)
Repairs for defects in materials or workmanship that the New Vehicle Limited Warranty would cover if the vehicle were within time/mileage. Excluded:
- insurance-paid body repairs; service contract repairs (incl. insurance-policy coverage)
- internal job cards / used-vehicle reconditioning
- government emission or safety inspections
- oil changes, tires, filters, fluid exchanges, belts
- other services called out as maintenance in the Owner Information booklet

Not excluded by name: alignments, brakes, batteries, bulbs.

## Submission mechanics
- Accounting copies showing actual labor time and labor dollars charged (parts: also part numbers, cost, sale). Job cards missing this are returned, not analyzed.
- Use the **Job Card Verification Form** from the Regional Warranty Team representative, or a GM-approved Excel sheet, listing every sequential job card with the ELR (or parts) calculation. Form not yet received.
- GM encourages delivery on a USB drive.
- Not stated: discount allocation method for labor, review turnaround, effective date.

## Parts reimbursement (Parts & Accessories manual, Art. 8)
- GM parts on warranty: dealer net price + the dealer's normal markup percentage allowance (standard % not printed in the sections reviewed).
- Parts from another GM-authorized source: lesser of invoice cost or dealer net, + normal markup.
- Non-GM vehicles / blank VIN parts warranty: dealer price + 30%.
- Core parts: allowance on dealer price only. ACDelco warranty parts and batteries at WD net (batteries + handling, capped at ACDelco DDC cost + normal markup). Tires through the GM Tire Program + normal markup. Non-GM parts: no markup.

## Audits and records
- SPPM 6.5 / DSSA 11.3: GM may audit and charge back improper transactions even if previously authorized. Records kept **24 months from job card date**.
- DSSA 7.1.4: GM pays parts plus a reasonable amount for labor per the SPPM. DSSA Article 16 sets GM's dispute resolution process.
