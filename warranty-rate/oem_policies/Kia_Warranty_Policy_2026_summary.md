# Kia – warranty labor rate (2026 forms) and Service Policies & Procedures Manual, Section 7

Sources (received from the store Nov 25, 2026; originals are dealer-confidential and not stored here):
- 2026 Kia Warranty Labor Rate Market Analysis form (PDF, 1 page)
- 2026 Kia Warranty Labor Rate RO Analysis Form WRTY101 (Excel)
- 2026 Kia Service Policies & Procedures Manual – **Section 7 only** (Warranty Repair Policies and Procedures, 7.0–7.24, 96 pages)

Findlay store: Findlay Kia (St George, UT). DealerUplift has filed for this store (labor and parts – see `history/`).

## Key points
- **Section 7 has no labor rate or parts markup policy.** It covers claim rules (gray market, PWA, goodwill, trained-tech edits, batteries, maintenance). The labor rate procedure itself is not in what we received; it may sit in another section of the manual or a separate bulletin. Ask the store/DPSM where the written procedure is.
- What the forms show:
  - **Method: "Effective Retail Labor Rate Method."** RO Analysis form has room for **100 customer-pay repair lines**: RO number, RO date, labor operation, labor hours (tenths), labor $, hourly rate, total. Labor time basis must be stated: Kia LTS, Chilton, or other. Also asks for the "Kiwi Effective Door Rate" and the current posted Kia customer retail labor rate.
  - **Market survey** (Market Analysis form): one dealer each from **Ford, GM, Honda, Hyundai, Mazda, Nissan, Toyota** – retail rate and warranty rate with effective dates, person contacted; average of warranty rates. Kia records the **District Average** and previous rate. New and existing dealers use the same form.
  - Approval chain: DPSM/RPSM -> Warranty Operations Manager -> Manager, Warranty Administration -> Director, Consumer Affairs & Warranty Operations -> Sr. Executive Coordinator. Form records approved/denied, approved rate, effective date.
  - No discount allocation formula, look-back window or sequence rule on the forms (the Excel has no formulas). Not stated whether the 100 lines must be consecutive.
- Parts: no markup procedure in Section 7. Goodwill is reimbursed "at warranty cost (parts, labor and parts handling)" (7.13) – Kia pays a parts handling allowance; rate not stated here.
- State law: 7.0 says the dealer must comply with federal, state and local warranty and consumer protection statutes; no statement on statutory rate filings.

## Section 7 items relevant to a statutory sample
- 7.21 Non-recommended services (nitrogen fills, engine flushes, injector cleaning, brake fluid flush, power transmission/coolant flushes, oil changes more often than the "normal" schedule) – Kia treats these as unnecessary; they are maintenance either way and out of a "warranty-like" sample.
- 7.20 Batteries: OE battery replacement in warranty reimbursed at 100% battery cost + labor. Under Utah 13-14-204 non-EV batteries are excluded from the statutory sample (VW accepted this, Nov 2026).
- 7.18 Trained Technician claim edit (PDI, A/C, A/T, electrical, engine management, engine mechanical, EVAP, HEV, EV) – warranty claims by untrained techs are denied; not a rate issue.
- Sublet: paid at actual invoice, no markup on sublet.
- Claims/chargebacks: supporting documentation must be retained; KUS may deny or charge back. No look-back period stated in Section 7.
