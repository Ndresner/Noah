# Toyota Warranty Policies 7.1–7.4 – labor rate (summary)

Source: Toyota Warranty Policy 7.1, 7.2, 7.3 (7/24) and 7.4 (1/26) PDFs, plus the Electronic Labor Rate Request Worksheet (Rev. 10-26), received Nov 2026 via Tyler Hughes (Toyota Flagstaff). Dealer-confidential manufacturer documents – summary and short quotes only; originals are not stored in the repo.

## Options (7.3 / 7.4)
- **Option A – CPI program:** automatic annual increase by calendar-year CPI-U, 2% minimum, initial 3-year term. Enrollment via the CPI Labor Rate Dealer Participation Agreement (check renewal / opt-out terms). Toyota Flagstaff: enrolled after its 2023 manual submission; 2026 is the last year of the term (per Tyler).
- **Option B – Effective labor rate:** worksheet + competitive market survey (nearest 4 dealers: 2 import, 2 domestic, none affiliated).

## Option B mechanics (7.4)
- Region sets the start date (an RO open date before the notice). From it, the dealer records the first 30 qualified Toyota customer-pay ROs in numeric order and "shall not bypass or omit any qualified RO." All ROs in the range (any make, any pay type) go in the packet.
- Customer-pay ROs within the most recent 120 days. Hours = hours billed to the customer, not flag time.
- ELR = net qualified labor $ / qualified hours. Discounts on an RO are allocated proportionally to all customer-paid labor and parts, then reduce the qualified labor.
- Once per rolling 12 months; requesting a start date and not completing (or declining the offer) can block a new request for 12 months (7.3). Rate capped at the Current Retail Labor Rate on the request form (7.3). Not eligible on WAS Tier 2/3; audit within 6 months can block (7.4, "unless prohibited by state law").
- Chargeback if a later inspection finds the warranty rate above the effective retail rate (7.2, 7.4).

## Non-qualified repairs (7.4 table) – highlights
- Excluded: scheduled maintenance per Toyota SMG (oil, filters, fluid services, spark plugs, ToyotaCare services), DIO accessories, cosmetic, sublet body/glass, undocumented diagnosis, extended warranty / third-party (whole RO), goodwill, hybrid battery fan/cooling service, inspections, outside-influence cleaning, non-genuine/used parts, tire work, non-Toyota vehicles, personalization, wear items (bulbs, fuses, other belts, wipers); internal, employee/family, rental, other-dealer/body-shop customers.
- Included exceptions: **alignments (no condition)**, brake pads/rotors, timing/alternator/serpentine belts, documented diagnosis (warning lights, squeak/rattle/leak), keys/fobs, radio/nav data patches, calibrations, damage-related mechanical repairs, rodent-damage repairs, TPMS (unless tied to recent tire work), clutch. "All other services are to be included."
- State law: 7.1 (flat-rate manual "except as required by State Law"); 7.4 limitations "unless prohibited by state law."
