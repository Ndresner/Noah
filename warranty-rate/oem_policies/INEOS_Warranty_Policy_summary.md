# INEOS – Warranty Policy and Procedures Manual §L1 (summary)

Source: INEOS Automotive Americas, LLC Warranty Policy and Procedures Manual, Version 1.10 (page footer reads "December 20243"; revision table on p.77 lists v1.0 Dec 2023 and v1.1 Dec 2024, "Addition of Distributor Markets"), received Nov 2026 for Findlay INEOS (NV). Copyrighted dealer document – summary and short quotes only; originals are not stored in the repo.

## Key points
- L1 (p.74) opens: "To extent allowed by applicable law, this section provides the procedures for calculating warranty labor and parts rates." The whole reimbursement section is subject to state law, but there is no Honda-style "alternative to your state statute" language.
- The manual does **not** state the labor-rate methodology. INEOS applies "the methodology described in the INEOS Labor Rate Review Form," which is not in the manual (L1.1.4: on the Portal; L1.5: in the Resource Center or from the Regional After Sales Manager). Get the form before planning a submission.
- L1.2 (p.75): parts are reimbursed "in accordance with applicable state law." No dealer-net-plus-X% handling figure, and no parts-markup request procedure anywhere in the manual. Parts markup looks like a statute-only path for INEOS.
- Labor = INEOS standard repair times x the authorized rate. The system caps payment at the published time (G2.1, p.53); labor "must not exceed INEOS's scheduled labor times and rates" (B1, p.14).
- Goodwill is paid at warranty rates for parts and labor, "not the INEOS Dealer retail rates." The dealer may not bill the customer the difference (J, p.65).

## L1.1 labor rate procedure
- **Who can ask:** Only an "Existing Dealer," meaning one that has operated as an INEOS Dealer for at least 12 months (L1.1.1). A New Dealer keeps the rate agreed in its application package for its first 12 months (L1.1.3). A Buying Dealer in a buy/sell inherits the seller's rate and also waits 12 months (L1.1.2).
- **Submission:** The INEOS Labor Rate Review Form, plus "the required materials identified in that application form," goes to the Warranty Manager, INEOS Warranty Department. The manual names no RO sample size, look-back window, RO-selection rule, exclusion list or market survey. All of those are in the form.
- **Decision:** INEOS notifies the dealer in writing (1) whether an adjustment is approved and (2) if so, the new rate. With an approval it sends an acknowledgment form for the dealer to sign. The manual gives no review deadline.
- **Effective date:** The new rate takes effect on the date the dealer returns the signed acknowledgment. It applies only to claims completed on or after that date, so nothing is retroactive.
- **Frequency:** After an approved adjustment, the dealer is "not eligible for another rate adjustment until 12 months after the effective date." The manual says nothing about a waiting period after a denied request.
- No eligibility screen tied to warranty GP% or retail ELR is stated, and no cap is stated. Any such tests would be in the form.

## Claims, audits, chargebacks
- **Submission:** Within 21 days of repair completion. The repair date is the technician's last clock time (G3.1, p.54). Queries or appeals are due within 28 days of INEOS's notice. Unpaid or unanswered claims must be raised within 60 days (G3.2).
- **Records:** Keep them at least 2 years after claim payment, including retail repair orders and parts sales invoices supporting the claims (E1.4, p.30). Displaced parts are held 180 days.
- **INEOS audits:** "All paid claims are subject to audit." Audits are normally sampled from claims paid in the previous 24 months. INEOS "reserves the right to conduct an extrapolation audit at any time," with chargebacks extrapolated over the audit period "to the extent allowed by applicable law" (E4, p.36). Labor is audited against technician clock times (F3.5, G2.2).
- **Self-audit:** Recommended every 3 months, or every 6 months per E2. It covers claims paid in the past 6 months, up to 50. Underpayments as well as overpayments should be reported to INEOS Warranty Operations (E2.1, p.31).
- **Other limits:** Prior authority (PWA) is needed for diagnosis over 1 hour, resubmitting denied claims, major units, tires and similar work (B1, p.18). Sublet may not exceed what the work would have cost in the dealer's own shop or the sublet's pre-tax invoice (L1.3). Tax is reimbursed where required (L1.4).

## Gaps / ambiguities
- The rate methodology, RO sample, exclusions and supporting materials are all in the Labor Rate Review Form, which is not in this manual. The manual also gives two locations for that form: the Portal (L1.1.4) and the Resource Center/RASM (L1.5).
- L1.1.1 says "See Section L1.4 Taxation," probably a mistaken reference to L1.1.4. "L1.3" is used twice, for Distributor Markets and Sublet.
- The version label is inconsistent: the footer says "1.10" and "December 20243," while the revision table says 1.1 / December 2024.
- Findlay INEOS (NV) must meet the 12-month Existing Dealer test, and so must a store acquired in a buy/sell. The store's INEOS start date and current authorized rate need to be confirmed.
