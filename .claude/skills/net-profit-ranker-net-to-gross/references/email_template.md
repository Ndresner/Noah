# Net Profit Ranker distribution email

Fill in `{Month}` (full name, e.g. October), `{Mon}` (e.g. Oct), `{YYYY}` and `{PY}` (the prior year). Send it from the user's account. Never mention AI.

**Subject:** {Month} Fixed Operations Net Profit Ranker

---

Hi all,

Attached are the {Month} Fixed Operations Net Profit Ranker reports. New this month: each report now has a second tab, the **Net to Gross Ranker**. It ranks every store by how much of its gross profit it keeps as net profit:

**Net to Gross % = Net Profit ÷ Total Gross Profit**

Net profit dollars naturally favor the bigger stores. Net to gross levels that out by showing how efficiently each store turns gross into profit, whatever its size. A store at 35% keeps 35 cents of net profit for every dollar of gross.

There are three versions of the report:

* **Service**: Service Department
* **Parts**: Parts Department
* **Combined (Total)**: Service and Parts together

**Net Profit Ranker tab (first tab)**

Net profit is what's left after a department's total gross profit is reduced by the expenses charged to that department:

**Net Profit = Total Gross Profit – Departmental Expenses**

For every store, the tab shows:

* **Monthly Net Profit**: January through {Month}
* **{YYYY} YTD Total and Monthly Average**
* **{YYYY} Pace**: the YTD monthly average annualized to project a full-year {YYYY} result
* **{PY} Final**: actual full-year net profit from last year
* **Pace +/- YOY and Pace % Chg**: how the projected {YYYY} pace compares with last year's actual result, in dollars and percentage

Stores are ranked from highest to lowest {YYYY} YTD net profit.

**Net to Gross Ranker tab (second tab)**

For every store, the tab shows:

* **Monthly Net to Gross %**: January through {Month}
* **{YYYY} YTD**: YTD net profit ÷ YTD gross profit
* **{PY} Final**: last year's full-year net to gross %
* **YTD +/- PY**: the change from last year, in percentage points

Stores are ranked from highest to lowest {YYYY} YTD net to gross %.

Both tabs have a total row for the group at the bottom.

Use the reports to track your store's net profit trend and to see how efficiently your departments turn gross into profit compared with the rest of the group. Please let me know if you have any questions.
