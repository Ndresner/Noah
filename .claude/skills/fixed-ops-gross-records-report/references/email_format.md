Plain text, no headings markup. Section titles sit on their own line. Bullets are `*`.

```
Subject: <Month> <Year> Fixed Ops Gross Profit Records

Team,
I want to take a moment to recognize another strong month across our Fixed Operations teams in <Month>. Thanks to the hard work, commitment, and collaboration of our Service Managers, Parts Managers, Advisors, Technicians, Parts Teams, General Managers, and support staff, <Month> landed as <accurate framing: Nth-best month in Findlay Automotive Group history / best <Month> ever, with context>.
As a group, here's where we finished:

* Service Gross: $<exact> – #<rank> all-time month
* Parts Gross: $<exact> – #<rank> all-time month
* Combined Service & Parts Gross: $<exact> – #<rank> all-time month

<One sentence on what the group result means (trend, consistency).>
Stores Setting New All-Time Combined Records
Congratulations to the following <N> dealerships for achieving the highest combined Service and Parts gross profit month in their history:

* <Store>

Additional Service Record Setters
Congratulations to the following store(s) for achieving a new all-time Service record:

* <Store not already listed above>

Additional Parts Record Setters
Congratulations to the following store(s) for achieving new all-time Parts Gross records:

* <Store not already listed above>

Outstanding Performances Across the Group
Several stores posted historically strong months, narrowly missing new records:

* <Store> posted its #2 all-time <Category> month ...
* <Stores> each posted their #3 all-time <Category> month

<Closing observation: one sentence on the pattern behind the results.>
Thank you to everyone who contributed to these results. Your leadership, teamwork, and commitment to delivering exceptional service to our customers continue to drive our success. Let's keep building on this into <next month>.
Great work, team.
```

Omit a record section entirely if nobody qualifies. Use "store" or "stores" to match the count.
