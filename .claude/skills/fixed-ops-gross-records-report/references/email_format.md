# Recognition email format (Aug 2026 email, record sections revised Nov 2026)

Plain text, no headings markup. Section titles sit on their own line. Bullets are `*`.

```
Subject: <Month> <Year> Fixed Ops Gross Profit Records

Team,
I want to take a moment to recognize another strong month across our Fixed Operations teams in <Month>. Thanks to the hard work, commitment, and collaboration of our Service Managers, Parts Managers, Advisors, Technicians, Parts Teams, General Managers, and support staff, <Month> landed as <accurate framing: Nth-best month in Findlay Automotive Group history / best <Month> ever, with context>.
As a group, here's where we finished:

* Service Gross: $<exact> – #<rank> all-time month
* Parts Gross: $<exact> – #<rank> all-time month
* Combined Service & Parts Gross: $<exact> – #<rank> all-time month

<One sentence on what the group result means (trend, consistency).>
Clean Sweep – Records in Service, Parts, and Combined
Congratulations to the following store(s) for setting new all-time records in Service, Parts, and Combined gross in the same month:

* <Store that set all three>

New All-Time Combined Records
Congratulations to the following <N> dealership(s) for achieving the highest combined Service and Parts gross profit month in their history:

* <Store> (<short note only when it matters, e.g. second straight month>)

New All-Time Service Records
Congratulations to the following Service department(s) for achieving the highest Service gross month in their store's history:

* <Store>

New All-Time Parts Records
Congratulations to the following Parts department(s) for achieving the highest Parts gross month in their store's history:

* <Store>

Outstanding Performances Across the Group
Several stores posted historically strong months, narrowly missing new records:

* <Store> posted its #2 all-time <Category> month ...
* <Stores> each posted their #3 all-time <Category> month

<Closing observation: one sentence on the pattern behind the results.>
Thank you to everyone who contributed to these results. Your leadership, teamwork, and commitment to delivering exceptional service to our customers continue to drive our success. Let's keep building on this into <next month>.
Great work, team.
```

Record sections are **one list per department**, so each department gets credit by name:
- A store appears in **every** list it earned. A store with Service + Combined records is listed under both. Never drop a store from a list because it's already in another.
- Every store is its own bullet. Never fold stores into a paragraph.
- Clean Sweep appears only when a store set all three records. Those stores are still listed in the three lists below it.
- Order stores in each list by the month's combined gross, the same order as the report.
- Notes in parentheses stay short and factual (repeat records, milestones like a first $2M month). Check every milestone against the Data tab.
- Leave out any section nobody qualifies for. Match singular or plural (store/stores, department/departments) to the count.
